How we choose.

Does it work in the UK and Ireland?

Does it write back to the CRM?

If the data ends up in a separate dashboard, it is harder to recommend.

Would we pay for it?

A pick means our own team uses it. Nobody pays to be listed. Product names and logos belong to their owners.
