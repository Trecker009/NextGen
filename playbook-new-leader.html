Playbooks

The new leader

A new VP reviews everything in the first 90 days. Be in the review.

Book a callAll playbooks
On this pageWhy it worksFind the accountsTime itThe callFollow upWatch out for

Why it works

A new sales or marketing leader reviews the team, tools and suppliers early. They want quick evidence that they were the right hire.

Find the accounts

  1. Track starts in VP Sales, CRO, CMO and RevOps roles across your list.
  2. Check where they came from and what they used there.
  3. Past users and buyers of yours go to the top.

Time it

WeekWhat they are doingWhat to do
1–2Meeting everyoneConnect on LinkedIn only
3–6Finding problemsCall with one specific observation
7–12Choosing what to changeOffer a short plan they can use

The call

“Hi Niamh, you’re about a month in, so I’ll be quick. I looked at how your team shows up in Ireland and there’s one thing I’d change. Want it now, or by email?”

Follow up

  1. Send the observation in three sentences, whether or not they ask.
  2. Call back a week later and ask what they found in their own review.
  3. Offer a pilot they can start and stop inside a quarter.

Watch out for

  • Internal promotions. They know the suppliers already, so change the angle.
  • Criticising their predecessor.

The hiring signal

A company advertising sales roles has budget, a target and a gap. Call before the hire starts.

The funding round

New money comes with a plan. Reach the person who has to deliver it.

The closed-lost revisit

Old losses are people who already know you. Ring when the reason has changed.
